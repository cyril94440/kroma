What a live stream shows where a film has its seek bar. At the live edge it only says so; behind it, it is the one control that goes back. It is a stop on the navigator like the bar it replaces, and `onFocus` / `onBlur` tell the player when the remote rests on it.

```tsx
<LiveBar
  behind={controller.live.behind}
  onGoLive={controller.live.goLive}
  onFocus={() => nav.focus('progress')}
  onBlur={() => nav.blur('progress')}
/>
```

<Scene name="Behind the edge" args={{ behind: true }}>
  The bar turns primary and offers the way back.
</Scene>

<Do>
- Set `flags.live` together with `controller.live`: the flag removes the seek bar, rewind and forward, and the controller carries what this bar shows.
</Do>

<Dont>
- Put a seek bar on a live stream. There is nothing ahead of the edge to seek to.
</Dont>
